Sihang Qiu is affiliated with NUDT and is a visiting researcher at Chinese Academy of Sciences (supervisor: prof. Daniel Dajun Zeng). He received his Ph.D. from the Web Information Systems group of TU Delft in 2021 (supervisors: prof. Geert-Jan Houben, prof. Alessandro Bozzon, dr. Ujwal Gadiraju). He holds a M.Sc. and a B.Eng. in Cybernetics. His research focuses on:

  • Crowdsourcing
  • Decision Intelligence
  • Social Simulation
  • Urban Management
He teaches:
  • Continuous System Simulation
He serves as a PC member of conferences such as WWW, AAAI, and RecSys; he is an Associate Editor of the journal Expert Systems with Applications.
