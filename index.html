SIHANG QIU

PhD candidate, Delft University of Technology [CV]

I am a PhD candidate at the Web Information Systems group of the Faculty of Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS/EWI), Delft University of Technology. My PhD topic focuses on Conversational Interfaces for Crowdsourcing. I also have broad interests in Mobile Crowdsensing and Human-Computer Interaction.
